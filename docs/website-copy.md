## PCMS — Polyclinic Management System

### Hero

Eyebrow: PCMS by Vektide

Headline: Run your polyclinic without the paperwork.

Subhead: One system for reception, laboratory and admin — so every patient visit, test and rupee is accounted for.

Buttons: Book a demo · See how it works

### The problem

Heading: Where clinics lose money.

Body: Consultation slips, lab registers and cash books don't talk to each other. Lab fees go unpaid, queues get messy, and at the end of the day nobody knows the real numbers.

### Built for every desk

Reception — Register visits, issue tokens, manage the queue and collect consultation fees in seconds.

Laboratory (add-on) — Record tests, track results and see exactly which lab payments are still pending.

Administrator — Daily income, pending dues and clinic activity on one dashboard. Full control over users and settings.

Doctors don't need to log in. They see patients; the system handles the rest.

### Key features

- Pending payment tracking — Never lose a lab fee again. Every unpaid amount is visible until it's settled.
- Token and queue management — Patients know their turn; reception stays calm.
- Separate money flows — Consultation and lab income tracked independently, reported together.
- Role-based access — Each staff member sees only what their job needs.
- Daily and monthly reports — Know your numbers without counting cash twice.
- Your own private system, managed by us — Your clinic gets its own system and its own database, kept completely separate from every other clinic. We handle hosting, updates, backups and security. Nothing to install.

### Core + add-ons

Heading: Start with the core. Add what you need.

Body: Every clinic gets the core system. Add modules now or later, and pay only for the ones you use. A new module goes live the same day, with no reinstall.

Core, included for every clinic:
- Consultation desk — Register visits, issue tokens, collect fees and refunds.
- Patients — Directory and full visit history.
- Doctors and services — Your doctors, fees and service list.
- Staff roles — Separate access for admin, reception and lab.
- Your clinic's name and logo — On screens, slips and receipts.

Add-on modules:
- Laboratory and diagnostics — Lab, X-ray, ultrasound and ECG: orders, sample queue, results and lab billing.
- Appointments — Book ahead, then check patients in with one click.
- Doctor payouts — Calculate what each doctor earned and record settlements.
- Staff and payroll — Staff directory, monthly salary slips and payroll.
- Staff schedules — Weekly shifts and duty rotas.
- Clinic expenses — Rent, electricity, supplies and other day-to-day spending.
- Doctor schedules — Weekly sitting hours, leaves and delays.
- Doctor presence — Show reception which doctors are in the clinic right now.

### Proof

Body: PCMS is live at Gorkhakali Polyclinic, built alongside the people who use it every day.

Testimonial: "[Quote from clinic owner/manager]" — [Name], [Role], Gorkhakali Polyclinic

### Getting started

1. Demo — We show you PCMS with your clinic's workflow in mind.
2. Setup — We set up your clinic's private system and configure doctors, tests, fees and staff accounts.
3. Training — Your team learns it in a day.
4. Go live — We stay on call while you switch over.

### Pricing line

Body: A one-time license for the core system and the modules you choose, plus a yearly plan for hosting, updates, backups and support. Billed in rupees. [Show price / "Contact us for pricing" — decide.]

### FAQ

- Do we need our own server? No. We host PCMS for you. Your clinic gets its own private system and database, and we set it up and maintain it.
- Is our data safe? Yes. Your clinic's data is kept in its own database, separate from every other clinic, with encrypted backups stored off-site. PCMS has no tracking and sends your patient and financial records nowhere. Your data belongs to you.
- Can it match how our clinic works? Yes. We adjust fees, tests, roles and workflows during setup.
- What if our internet goes down? PCMS runs online, so it needs a connection. We help you set up a backup connection, like a 4G router, and a simple paper fallback. Anything written on paper is entered once you're back online.
- What if something breaks? You call us directly, and we fix most issues remotely, without a site visit. [Support hours.]
