We host your system for you, on servers we manage, with your own separate database. A yearly hosting plan covers the server, security updates, monitoring and backups.

- We aim for your system to be available at least 99% of each month. This is a target, not a guarantee.
- We give at least 24 hours' notice of planned maintenance, and schedule it outside your working hours where we can. Urgent security fixes may happen without notice.

You're responsible for your own internet connection, computers, printers and browsers.

### 13. Fees

- We issue a tax invoice under our registered name and PAN for every payment.
- The hosting plan is billed yearly in advance. We give at least 30 days' written notice before changing its price, and the change applies from your next renewal.
- A license fee, once paid, never changes.
