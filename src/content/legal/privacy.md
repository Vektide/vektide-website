## This website

### 1. Who we are

Vektide Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Vektide", "we") is a software company registered in Nepal, with its office at Mahalaxmi-4, Lalitpur. We are responsible for the information described in this part of the policy.

This policy is written with Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 and Electronic Transactions Act, 2063 in mind.

### 2. What this policy covers

It has two parts:

- **This website** covers vektide.com and anything you send us by form or email.
- **Systems we run for clients** covers the data our clients keep in the software we build and host for them, such as PCMS.

### 3. What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us, plus the basic records any website produces.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Contact form | What your message is about, your name, your business, your phone number, your email (optional) and your message |
| Demo form | Your name, your business, what you'd like to see, your phone number, your email (optional) and an optional note |
| Email | Whatever you tell us, and your address so we can reply |
| Visiting the site | Your IP address, browser, the page you asked for and the time. Our hosting provider records this in its server logs, and your browser sends your IP address to Fontshare when it loads our fonts |

Please don't send patient records or other sensitive personal information through our forms. If we need it, we'll agree a safer way.

### 4. How we use it

We use what you send us to:

- reply to your message;
- arrange and run a demo;
- prepare a quote; and
- keep a record of what we've discussed, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

We don't add you to a mailing list, use your details for advertising, or pass them to anyone to sell to you.

### 5. Cookies and browser storage

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't store anything in your browser. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

### 6. Who else handles it

A few services help us run this site. They see only what they need for their part.

| Service | What it does | What it sees |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel | Hosts this website (our current provider) | Server logs: IP address, browser, page and time |
| Web3Forms | Delivers form messages to our inbox | Everything you type into a form |
| Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry) | Serves the site's fonts | Your IP address and browser |
| Our business email provider | Receives and stores our email | Messages you send us, and form messages |

If we change any of these providers, we'll update this table.

### 7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for [2 years] after we last hear from you, then delete them. If you become a client, we keep what we need for as long as we work together and for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for its own short retention period.

## Systems we run for clients

### 8. The client owns the data

When we build and host a system for a business, that business decides what goes into it and why. It owns the data. We store and process it only to run the system, keep it safe and give support, and only on the client's instructions.

If the data is about you, for example as a patient at a clinic, the business you dealt with is responsible for it. See [section 16](#16-if-youre-a-patient-or-customer-of-one-of-our-clients).

### 9. Every client's system is separate

Each client gets its own system, its own database, its own backups and its own settings. One client can never see another client's data.

### 10. What we do with it, and what we never do

We use client data only to:

- keep the system running, secure and backed up;
- fix problems the client reports, and apply updates; and
- restore data after an incident, or when the client asks.

We will never:

- sell, rent or trade any client data;
- use it for advertising, marketing or profiling;
- analyse it for our own purposes, or combine it with other clients' data; or
- use it to contact our clients' patients or customers.

### 11. Where it's stored

The live system runs on a server we rent in a professional data centre. Encrypted backups are kept with a separate storage provider, so losing the main server doesn't lose the backups. For PCMS today, these are OVHcloud in Gravelines, France for the server, and Cloudflare R2 in the European Union for backups. Traffic passes through Cloudflare's network, which filters attacks but stores no records.

These providers can be outside Nepal. Wherever the data is held, this policy applies in full, and we tell clients in writing at least 30 days before moving their data to a different provider or country. Each product's own data privacy policy lists its providers in detail.

Client data is never kept on our laptops, phones or personal cloud storage.

### 12. Who on our team can access it

Only Vektide's two directors can reach our servers. Any future team member will get access only after signing a confidentiality agreement, and only as far as their job needs.

Routine work such as updates, monitoring and checking backups doesn't involve opening individual records. We look at a client's records only to fix a problem they've reported, restore data or prepare an export they've asked for. In an emergency, like an active attack, we act first and tell the client straight away what we did.

### 13. How we protect it

- All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Nobody, including us, can read them.
- Every action is checked against the user's role on the server, not just in the browser.
- Repeated login attempts are slowed down to stop password guessing.
- The database can't be reached from the internet.
- Server access uses key-based login over a private encrypted network, and our provider accounts use two-factor authentication.
- Security updates are applied promptly.
- Backups are encrypted before they leave the server. The key is never stored with them.
- We rehearse a full restore at least once a year.

No system is perfectly secure, so we review these measures regularly and improve them as we go.

### 14. Backups and retention

We never delete a client's records automatically. The client decides how long to keep them. Backups give us points in time we can restore to:

| Restore point | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Hourly | 24 hours |
| Daily | 14 days |
| Weekly | 8 weeks |
| Monthly | 12 months |

Older restore points are deleted automatically. Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days.

### 15. If something goes wrong

If client data is accessed, changed, disclosed or lost without permission, we:

1. stop it, and change any affected passwords and keys;
2. tell the client within 72 hours of finding out, with what we know so far;
3. restore service, and restore data from the last clean backup where needed; and
4. give the client a written report within 14 days: what happened, what was affected, why, and what we've changed.

The client, as owner of the data, decides whether patients, staff or authorities need to be told. We give them the facts and any help they need.

### 16. If you're a patient or customer of one of our clients

Please contact that business directly. It holds your records and can show or correct them. If your request reaches us, we pass it to the business within 2 working days and help them answer it.

### 17. When a client leaves

- A client can ask for a full export of its data at any time.
- When hosting ends, we give the client a complete export within 30 days of asking.
- Once the client confirms it has the export, or 90 days after hosting ends, whichever is first, we delete its database and confirm that in writing.
- Remaining encrypted backups expire on their normal schedule, so no copy is left after 12 months at most.

## For everyone

### 18. Your rights

You can ask us:

- what information we hold about you;
- to correct anything that's wrong or incomplete;
- how we use it and who we share it with; and
- to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it, for example for tax records.

Email us and we'll reply within one working day and act within 30 days. If your data is in a client's system, see [section 16](#16-if-youre-a-patient-or-customer-of-one-of-our-clients).

### 19. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date and version at the top. If a change reduces the protection of a client's data, we tell that client in writing at least 30 days before, and the change needs their agreement under their contract.
